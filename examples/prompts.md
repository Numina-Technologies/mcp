# Prompts to try

Connect the Numina MCP first, then ask:

**Overview**
- What was our result last month, and how does it compare to the same month last year?
- Which five expense accounts grew the most this year?
- What's our bank balance right now?

**Invoices**
- Which customer invoices are overdue, and by how much?
- How much have we invoiced Acme ApS this year?

**Documents**
- Find the receipt for the Adobe charge in March.
- Which bank transactions last month have no receipt?

**Close and VAT**
- Show the trial balance at the end of the last quarter.
- How much output and input VAT did we have last quarter?

**Bookkeeping** (drafts only, you approve them in Numina)
- Go through last week's unreconciled bank lines and draft them.
- Attach this receipt to the Staples payment and fix the VAT code.
- What's still sitting unbooked in my drafts?
